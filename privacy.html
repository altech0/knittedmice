Privacy Policy

Queens · Altech · Last updated: May 2026

Queens is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any personal information. The only thing we store is a nickname you choose yourself.

Who We Are

Queens is developed by Altech, based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at privacy@altech.dev.

What We Collect

When you first open Queens, you choose a nickname. That nickname is stored on our servers so you can be identified within the app. We also store:

  • A randomly generated token used to authenticate your requests to our servers
  • A device identifier derived from Apple's App Attest service, used once during registration to verify you are using a genuine copy of Queens on a real Apple device. It is not used to track you.
  • The date your account was created

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, IP address, location, or any other personal information.

Why We Collect It

  • Nickname — so you have an identity within the app
  • Auth token — so the app can securely fetch puzzles from our servers
  • Device identifier — to prevent automated abuse of our puzzle service. It is checked once at registration and not used again.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone
  • We do not share your data with third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not track your behaviour inside the app
  • We do not use analytics services

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored in a database hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Cloudflare is a US-based company operating data centres globally. By using Queens you agree to your data being stored on Cloudflare's servers. You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

How Long We Keep It

Your account data is kept for as long as you use the app. If you delete your account, all data associated with it is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen inside the app. This permanently removes your nickname, auth token, and device identifier from our servers.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK resident you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@altech.dev.

Children

Queens does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has registered, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
